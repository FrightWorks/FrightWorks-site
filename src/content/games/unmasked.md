A native PC port of the 2005 GameCube game, made the same way as Night of 100 Frights: its PowerPC code is translated ahead of time to C++ with [WiiCompiled](https://github.com/patchzyy/Wiicompiled), so there's no emulator in between. In the works: up to 4K, widescreen up to 2.39:1, 120 or 180 fps with frame interpolation, HDR, and gamepads with matching button prompts. You'll need your own copy of the GameCube USA disc (G5DE78).

In the original, the gang visits Fred's cousin Jed at a special-effects studio, finds him missing and his animatronic monsters running wild, and follows the trail through Chinatown and beyond.
