## Features

- Up to 4K, true widescreen (16:9, fill, cinematic 2.39:1) or the original 4:3
- 30 or 60 fps, MSAA, HDR with brightness calibration
- Motion blur and cartoon ink outlines, plus experimental ambient occlusion and bloom
- The Creeper window animation from the Xbox edition, back in Mystic Manor's courtyard
- Built-in HD texture pack by dogerki (can be switched off)
- Gamepads and keyboard, remappable in the launcher or in game (**F10**). Button prompts match your Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo controller
- Optional original Xbox button layout, with matching prompts and tutorial hints, picked automatically for Xbox controllers if you like
- The launcher works fully with a controller
- GameCube memory-card saves; saves from Refrightened and earlier versions are brought over
- Skip the intro movies; mods that replace game files or textures, or [add things to a level](https://github.com/FrightWorks/FrightWorks-no100f/blob/main/docs/mod-patches.md)
- Holiday events follow the real date, or pick one (or confetti fireworks) on the launcher's **Extras** page
- Optional: live story cutscenes, uncompressed textures and hiss-free sound from your own [PS2 or Xbox disc](#ps2-and-xbox-extras)

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or Linux x86_64 for the beta
- Your own copy of **Night of 100 Frights, GameCube USA v1.01 (GIHE78, revision 1)** as `.iso`, `.gcm` or `.ciso`

No game data is included. The launcher reads everything from your disc image.

## Installing on Windows

1. Download `FrightWorks-win-Setup.exe`. It installs for your Windows user, with no admin rights, and adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts. Prefer one folder? Unzip the `.zip` anywhere instead.
2. Open **FrightWorks** and press **Play**. The first time, it asks for your disc image.

Windows may warn that the setup is from an unknown publisher, because it isn't code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Linux (beta)

The AppImage runs on any x86_64 Linux with glibc 2.34 or newer: Ubuntu 22.04 and newer, Debian 12, Fedora, Arch and SteamOS. It needs a Vulkan driver, and FUSE to start (`fusermount` or `fusermount3`, which desktops usually have).

```sh
chmod +x FrightWorks.AppImage
./FrightWorks.AppImage
```

Without FUSE, run `./FrightWorks.AppImage --appimage-extract-and-run`. Saves, settings and files from your disc are in `~/.local/share/WiiCompiled`; screenshots and videos go to `FrightWorks` in your Pictures and Videos folders. Video recording and the fullscreen monitor choice aren't there yet on Linux. Tell us how it goes on your setup in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/FrightWorks/FrightWorks-no100f/issues).

## Updates

The installed game and the AppImage look for a new version when the launcher starts and ask before updating; **Extras > Updates** turns that off or checks right away. Updates keep your saves and settings. The zip can't update itself: its launcher tells you when a release is out, and you unzip the new one over the old folder.

## Where your files are

| | Installed | Zip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saves | `Saved Games\FrightWorks` | `data\UserData\Saves` |
| Settings, mods, logs, files from your disc | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FrightWorks-Data` | `data\UserData`, `data\Disc` |
| Screenshots (**F2**) / videos (**F3**) | `Pictures\FrightWorks` / `Videos\FrightWorks` | `Screenshots` / `Videos` beside the launcher |

**Extras > Folders** in the launcher opens these folders, can move your saves somewhere that's backed up, and copies saves and settings over from an earlier FrightWorks or Refrightened install.

## PS2 and Xbox extras

If you own the PS2 or Xbox version of the game, FrightWorks can take a few things from your own disc image that the GameCube disc lacks or stores at lower quality. On the launcher's **Extras** page, press **Choose PS2 or Xbox disc image** and pick a plain `.iso` dump of the disc (Xbox: a trimmed "xiso" or a full dump). **Remove** takes it all out again.

| | PS2 disc | Xbox disc |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live story cutscenes instead of the GameCube's movies | 13 | 14, with the ending |
| Textures without the GameCube's compression | | about 1,860 |
| Music, voices and effects without the GameCube's hiss | | about 1,000 sounds |

The cutscenes are rendered live at your resolution instead of the movies' 640x448, and any scene your disc doesn't have plays the GameCube movie. The Xbox textures load after the HD pack: with HD textures on, they fill in what the HD pack doesn't cover. Everything copied stays on your PC, and the PS2 and Xbox versions themselves can't be played. [More about the extras](https://github.com/FrightWorks/FrightWorks-no100f/blob/main/docs/ps2-xbox-extras.md).

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Which disc do I need?</summary>

The GameCube USA disc, version 1.01: Game ID **GIHE78**, revision **1**, as an `.iso`, `.gcm` or `.ciso` image of your own disc. Redump lists it as "Scooby-Doo! Night of 100 Frights (USA) (Rev 1)".

USA version 1.00 and the European (PAL) GameCube release aren't supported yet but might be later. The PS2 and Xbox versions can't be played with FrightWorks, but your own PS2 or Xbox disc image can add their cutscenes, textures and sound: see [PS2 and Xbox extras](#ps2-and-xbox-extras).

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I tell which version my disc image is?</summary>

The launcher checks it for you: when you choose a disc image, it tells you if the file isn't a GameCube image, is a different game or region (another Game ID, such as the European `GIHP78`), or is version 1.00 (revision 0).

To check before you choose it, in [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org) right-click the game > **Properties** > **Info**: it shows the **Game ID** (`GIHE78`) and **Revision** (`1`).

To verify a dump, a plain `.iso` of the right disc is 1,459,978,240 bytes with SHA-1 `cf5638fd61a7862e07066fb290381bdfc77d1f37` (MD5 `6dca7f5ed6b400c8216526107e83f3cd`, CRC-32 `b29a2a47`), the same as Redump's. A `.ciso` hashes differently even though it works.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My disc image is an .rvz (or NKit). Will it work?</summary>

Not as it is. The launcher takes a plain `.iso` or `.gcm`, or a `.ciso`. Compressed `.rvz`, `.gcz` and `.wia` images, NKit images (`.nkit.iso`, `.nkit.gcz`) and Wii images don't work.

Dolphin turns an `.rvz` into a plain image: right-click the game > **Convert File...** > **Format: ISO**. It can't do that for NKit images: rebuild those with the NKit tool you made them with.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the game included in the download?</summary>

No. No game data is included. The first time you press **Play**, the launcher asks for your disc image and reads everything from it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Windows says the setup is from an unknown publisher. Is that expected?</summary>

Yes. The setup isn't code-signed, so Windows warns about it. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it run on Linux or SteamOS?</summary>

There is a beta AppImage for x86_64 Linux with glibc 2.34 or newer, which covers Ubuntu 22.04 and newer, Debian 12, Fedora, Arch and SteamOS. See [Linux (beta)](#linux-beta) for what it needs.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I played Scooby-Doo: Refrightened. Can I keep my saves?</summary>

Yes. On first start, the launcher looks for an earlier FrightWorks zip or Refrightened folder (beside the new zip, or for the installed game in Downloads, Desktop and Documents) and offers to copy your saves, settings, controls, mods and disc files over. The old folder is left as it is, and old saves are converted with a backup. If it isn't found, use **Extras > Folders > Copy from an earlier version**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does uninstalling delete my saves?</summary>

No. Uninstalling from Windows Settings > Apps removes the program only. Your saves and settings stay.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change settings or controls?</summary>

In the launcher under **Settings**, or in game with **F10** (or the controller's **Back**/**Select**, as long as no mapping uses it). **F11** toggles fullscreen. With more than one monitor, **Settings > Display > Fullscreen monitor** picks the one fullscreen uses. More in the [player's guide](https://github.com/FrightWorks/FrightWorks-no100f/blob/main/docs/playing.md).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Something isn't working. Where do I get help?</summary>

Open an issue in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/FrightWorks/FrightWorks-no100f/issues) and say what happened on your setup.

</details>
