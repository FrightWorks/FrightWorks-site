A port of the 2004 GameCube game is planned after Unmasked. Details will appear here once work starts.

In the original, someone has freed thousands of ghosts and monsters from a magic book, the Tome of Doom. As Scooby and Shaggy, you find clues and capture the monsters across five locations, from Hambridge Library to a bayou.
